I completed my M.Tech in Geomatic Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines), Dhanbad, where my thesis developed a decadal, multi-sensor framework for prioritizing ecological restoration across the Jharia Coalfield. I am currently pursuing a **Ph.D. in Mining Engineering at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad** and completing first-year doctoral coursework under the supervision of **Prof. Dheeraj Kumar**.

My research increasingly focuses on developing spatially rigorous Earth-observation and AI methods for **landslide susceptibility and deformation monitoring**, **environmental assessment of mining landscapes**, and related geospatial problems where data scarcity, spatial dependence, and physical consistency matter.

## Research focus

### Geohazards: from susceptibility to deformation
My Sikkim research progresses from knowledge-driven susceptibility modelling to hybrid statistical methods, spatially validated ensemble learning, and finally multi-temporal InSAR deformation monitoring. This provides a continuous research thread from **where failure is likely** to **how active slopes are moving through time**.

### Spatially rigorous GeoAI
I am interested in machine-learning and generative approaches that respect geography rather than treating spatial observations as interchangeable tabular rows. Current work examines synthetic-data augmentation for landslide inventories, with particular attention to spatial leakage, geographically inconsistent covariates, and reliable evaluation.

### Mining-environment monitoring
My M.Tech thesis fused Sentinel-1/2, Landsat-8/9, and VIIRS observations across a ten-year archive to build a 10 m restoration-priority framework for the ~370 km² Jharia Coalfield. The work introduced **Vol-NDVI** and **Surface Alteration Surface (SAS)** for mining-landscape assessment and used Fuzzy AHP for indicator weighting.

## Selected research and outputs

### Multi-Temporal InSAR Landslide Monitoring · Gangtok, Sikkim
Processed **31 ascending + 24 descending Sentinel-1A acquisitions**, generating **200 interferometric pairs** and approximately **10 million coherent pixels** using ISCE2 and MintPy. Joint inversion decomposed LOS velocities into east-west and vertical displacement components, while time-series analysis captured clear monsoon-period acceleration. *Manuscript ready for submission.*

### Jharia Coalfield Restoration Prioritization · M.Tech Thesis
Developed the complete research workflow, from study design and multi-sensor processing to index development, weighting, validation, and manuscript preparation. The resulting Restoration Priority Index achieved **Moran's I = 0.8831** and **ROC-AUC = 0.8355**. *First-author manuscript under review at Science of Remote Sensing.*

### Landslide Susceptibility Modelling · Sikkim Himalaya
A sequence of studies spanning AHP, FR, logistic regression, hybrid models, XGBoost, and Weighted Subspace Random Forest. The latest ensemble study uses **five-fold spatial cross-validation** and explicitly evaluates the trade-off between predictive sensitivity and zonation efficiency.

### Peer-Reviewed Publication · Earth Surface Processes and Landforms
Co-authored *A comparative analysis of AHP, FR, AHP-FR and LR models for landslide susceptibility mapping in Sikkim Himalaya, India* (2026), evaluating deterministic, statistical, and hybrid susceptibility models in a data-scarce Himalayan setting.
