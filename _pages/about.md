    <p class="eyebrow-v2">RESEARCH PROFILE</p>
    <h2>Earth observation that connects prediction with physical change.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="intro-grid-v2">
    <p>I completed my M.Tech in Geomatic Engineering at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, where I developed a decadal, multi-sensor framework for ecological restoration prioritization in the Jharia Coalfield. I am currently pursuing a <strong>Ph.D. in Mining Engineering</strong> at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad under the supervision of <strong>Prof. Dheeraj Kumar</strong>.</p>
    <p>My current research moves from <strong>landslide susceptibility</strong> toward <strong>active deformation monitoring</strong>, while also investigating spatially rigorous machine learning and synthetic-data methods that preserve geographic and physical consistency.</p>
