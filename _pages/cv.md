## Research Profile

I work at the intersection of **Geospatial AI, Earth observation, InSAR, and spatial modelling**, with applications to geohazards, mining-environment monitoring, and Earth-surface processes. My research combines multi-source satellite observations with spatial statistics and machine learning, with particular interest in deformation time series, spatially rigorous validation, and physically consistent geospatial AI.

*Thesis:* *Multi-Temporal and Spatial Autocorrelation-Based Assessment of Mining-Induced Ecological Degradation Using a Restoration Priority Index: A Case Study of the Jharia Coalfield, India.*

Developed a decadal restoration-prioritization framework for the ~370 km² Jharia Coalfield using a ten-year multi-sensor satellite archive. Introduced two novel indices for mining-landscape assessment, **Vol-NDVI** and **Surface Alteration Surface (SAS)**, and integrated six degradation indicators using Fuzzy AHP. The final index achieved Moran's I = **0.8831** and ROC-AUC = **0.8355**.

## Selected Research Experience

### Multi-Temporal InSAR Landslide Monitoring · Gangtok, Sikkim
*Mar 2026 - Present · IIT (ISM) Dhanbad*

Processed 31 ascending and 24 descending Sentinel-1A acquisitions using ISCE2 and MintPy, producing 200 interferometric pairs and approximately 10 million coherent pixels. Joint inversion decomposed LOS velocities into east-west and vertical displacement components; time-series analysis captured monsoon-period acceleration. **All software implementation, InSAR processing, coding, and computational analysis were completed by me.** Manuscript ready for submission.

### Synthetic Data for Landslide Inventories
*Apr 2026 - Present · Research in progress*

Investigating geo-conditional generative modelling and the validity of synthetic augmentation for landslide inventories, with emphasis on geographic consistency, spatial leakage, and leakage-safe evaluation. The project originated from GCT-TabDDPM and is being refined as the methodological framework develops. **Research design, implementation, and code developed independently.**

### Jharia Coalfield Restoration Prioritization · M.Tech Thesis
*Jan 2025 - Apr 2026 · IIT (ISM) Dhanbad*

Designed and implemented the complete research workflow, including multi-sensor data processing, index development, Fuzzy AHP weighting, trend analysis, spatial validation, sensitivity analysis, and manuscript preparation. Validation: **Moran's I = 0.8831; ROC-AUC = 0.8355; Monte Carlo CV = 2.56%; coal-fire capture = 77.48%.** First-author manuscript under review at *Science of Remote Sensing*.

### Landslide Susceptibility Modelling · Sikkim Himalaya
*Aug 2024 - Present · IIT (ISM) Dhanbad*

Research program spanning AHP susceptibility mapping, deterministic/statistical/hybrid model comparison, and ensemble machine learning under five-fold spatial cross-validation. Latest benchmark: **XGBoost AUC = 0.895; sensitivity = 0.955**; WSRF captured **84.5% of landslides within 28.2% of the study area**.

### Hyperspectral Mineral Mapping · Bailadila Iron Ore Mine
*Summer 2025 · Independent research*

Built an EO-1 Hyperion processing workflow using FLAASH atmospheric correction, PCA/MNF reduction, and Spectral Angle Mapper classification for hematite, pyrite, and quartz-associated signatures.

### Data Science Intern · TEXMiN, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad
*Mar 2025 - Aug 2025*

Built Python machine-learning workflows for extracting environmental degradation metrics from high-resolution satellite imagery in support of data-driven mine monitoring.
